Ad exchanges work best when your ad is clear and you stay consistent. Try these:

1. Offer one thing, not five

Send people to one page with one clear action. A confused visitor leaves.

2. Write a headline that says what they get

"Free guide: 7 ways to..." beats "Click here." Say the benefit in plain words.

3. Use a simple landing page

Big headline, one button, and an email box. Load speed matters on phones.

4. Capture emails, not just clicks

Most visitors only come once. An email address lets you reach them again.

5. Be consistent

Fill every ad slot and keep them filled. Small daily traffic adds up.

6. Track what works

Use a different link tag for each ad so you can see which one gets clicks.

7. Follow up with value

Send useful tips first. Sell second. People unsubscribe from pure pitches.
